Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Who we are. Sacrera Sacred Geometry (“Sacrera”, “the app”) is made by Daniel Yordanov (“we”), 3 Patriarh Evtimii St, 9900 Novi Pazar, Bulgaria. Contact: sacrera.app@gmail.com. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

In short. Your practice stays on your phone. The app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We never receive your drawings, your journey progress, your answers, your journal, your birth details or your reminder time. We receive a record of your purchases, and your email address only if you choose to give it to us.

What stays on your phone. The app keeps these in its private storage on your device and never sends them to us:

  • your journey progress, daily practice set and history, and 30-day challenge
  • your answers to “How close do you feel to your inner world?” and “What brings you here?”
  • your reminder time, favorite meditations, recently played meditations, sleep ratings, settings and chosen look
  • the tarot cards you draw and your notes on them
  • your dream journal, what you write and seal in Release, and your affirmations and how true they feel to you
  • the birth date, time and town you enter for your birth chart, and the town you pick for tonight’s sky
  • drawings and forms you create, and pictures you draw in Unwind

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, deletes it from the phone. If backup is on for your Google account, Android may include the app’s data in your phone’s backup to Google Drive. Google keeps that backup under your account; we can’t see it. The backup includes your journal and your birth details; if you would rather they stay only on the phone, turn off backup in your phone’s settings.

Files you save. Videos, wallpapers and drawings are saved to your phone’s storage only when you tap Save.

Reminders. Android schedules reminders on your phone. The app asks for permission to send notifications when you set a reminder, or when you start a free trial, so it can remind you two days before the trial ends.

Email, only if you ask. The app never asks for your email to work. In a few places (and in Settings › By email) you can ask us to send you the printable sheets by email, and tick a separate box if you also want news of new journeys and apps, about once a month. If you do:

  • we receive your email address, the date, which of the two you asked for, and where in the app you asked
  • we first send one message asking you to confirm the address. If you don’t confirm it, it isn’t added to our list
  • we send the sheets, and the news only if you ticked that box. Every message has a link to unsubscribe in one tap
  • we keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe we keep only a record that you did, so we never write to you again
  • our email service, Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), stores the list and sends the messages for us, under a data processing agreement. Its own handling is described at brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy

Purchases. You buy through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see your card. Google gives us an order number, the plan, the date, the price and the country of purchase. To check your purchase and keep your access when you reinstall or change phones, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives a random ID the app makes for you (not your name or email), your purchase history from Google Play, technical details such as the app version, your phone’s system and your store country, and, as any server does, your IP address. It gives us totals of purchases, trials and renewals. We use all this to give you access, to handle refunds, to understand how many people buy and keep a plan, and to keep the records Bulgarian accounting and tax law require, for as long as that law requires. RevenueCat works for us under a data processing agreement; because it is based in the United States, your data may be transferred there under the safeguards the GDPR requires. Google’s own handling is described at policies.google.com/privacy, RevenueCat’s at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Messages you send us. If you write to sacrera.app@gmail.com, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and delete it two years after the conversation ends, unless the law requires us to keep it. Our mail is a Gmail account, so Google stores these messages for us, as our service provider.

Crash and performance reports. If you agreed in your phone’s settings to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may show us crash and performance reports about the app. They don’t identify you.

Legal bases. We process purchase data, including the checks through RevenueCat, to give you what you bought (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), count purchases, trials and renewals because we have a legitimate interest in understanding how the app is doing (art. 6(1)(f)), keep accounting records because the law requires it (art. 6(1)(c)), answer your messages because we have a legitimate interest in helping you (art. 6(1)(f)), and send you the sheets and any news because you asked for them (your consent, art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw that consent at any time by unsubscribing; it doesn’t affect what was sent before.

Sharing. We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone beyond the services named above (Google, RevenueCat and Brevo), which work for us.

Security. The app itself sends nothing over the internet except purchase checks with Google Play and RevenueCat, and your email address to our email service if you ask for the sheets, over an encrypted connection. Data on your phone is protected by Android’s app storage, which other apps can’t read.

Children. The app is made for adults, 18 and over. It isn’t aimed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their email address, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights. You can ask us for access to your data, and to correct, delete, restrict or transfer it, or object to how we use it. Write to sacrera.app@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни), 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd, Sofia 1592, cpdp.bg, or to the authority in your own EU country.

Changes. If this policy changes, we post the new version here with a new date, and tell you in the app if the change matters to you.

Questions: sacrera.app@gmail.com
