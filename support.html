Support

Write any time to sacrera.app@gmail.com.

Cancel the yearly plan

In Google Play, open Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and choose Sacrera. You keep the full version until the end of the year you paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the plan.

Restore a purchase

Sign in to Google Play with the account you bought with, open the app and tap Restore purchase.

The free trial

The 7-day trial of the yearly plan is free and can be cancelled any time before it ends. The app reminds you two days before. If you cancel, you keep the full version until the trial ends, and everything you made stays.

Refunds

Google Play can refund some purchases itself shortly after you buy. After that, write to us and tell us what happened.

Your data

Your practice stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it. If you joined the email list, every email has an unsubscribe link, or write to us and we’ll delete your address. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Something doesn’t work

Tell us your phone model, your Android version and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps.
